# Code of conduct

Scany is a small personal project. Discussion stays on the code, the
scan quality, and the privacy behavior.

- Be direct and specific. Point at a file, a screen, or a repro.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or unsolicited commercial pitches.
- Do not post other people’s documents, tokens, or account data in
  issues or pull requests. Screenshots of a scan should be a page you
  are willing to publish.

Reports: [prnysoni@gmail.com](mailto:prnysoni@gmail.com). The maintainer
may hide or lock a thread that breaks these rules.
