Support

User support for Scany on iPhone

Email prnysoni@gmail.com for app help, privacy questions, or to request deletion of a connected session (disconnect Google Drive or iCloud in the app Settings, which removes tokens stored on the device).

Google Drive access

Scany asks Google only for files this app creates and for your email so the account label in Settings is correct. You can revoke access any time at Google Account permissions, then tap Disconnect in Scany.

Requirements

  • iPhone 12 or newer
  • iOS 15 or later
  • Camera permission for scanning
